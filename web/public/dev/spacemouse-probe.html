SpaceMouse probe

The tuning and regression tool for dev/plan-spacemouse.md. It opens the puck over WebHID and says exactly what it sends: which report IDs arrive, how long each one is, what its bytes decode to under each candidate layout, and how far the axes travel at full deflection. Nothing here touches the app, and nothing happens until you press Connect: WebHID needs a user gesture, by design.
