Aggregate

Build aggregate distributions from DecL and read them in cells.

=AGG.BUILD(decl, [log2], [bs])
A handle such as agg.A#4f40714271198d8f.
=AGG.EXHIBITS(handle)
The exhibits it can show.
=AGG.EXHIBIT(handle, name, [perspective], [block], [headers])
One exhibit, as values.
=AGG.Q(handle, p)
Quantiles; p a number or a range in [0, 1].
=AGG.VERSION()
The server's version and the library's.

References resolve to library entries only. Combine your own programs through text: =AGG.BUILD("port P " & A1 & " " & B1).

A refused program or argument comes back as #AGG! and the reason, as text you can read in the cell. Only a transient failure (unreachable, rate limited, timed out) shows #VALUE!.

Every program goes to the server and into its audit log. On a public server, never put real data in DecL.
